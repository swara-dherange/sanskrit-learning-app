Articulation Groups

स्थानम् — Place of Articulation
Learn where the sound is produced in the vocal tract.

Kaṇṭhya — Guttural

कण्ठ्य

These sounds are produced mainly from the throat.

📍 Place: Throat

Examples: क ख ग घ ङ

Tālavya — Palatal

तालव्य

These sounds are produced with the tongue approaching or contacting the palate.

📍 Place: Palate

Examples: च छ ज झ ञ

Mūrdhanya — Retroflex

मूर्धन्य

These sounds are produced with the tongue curled toward the upper part of the mouth.

📍 Place: Retroflex region

Examples: ट ठ ड ढ ण

Dantya — Dental

दन्त्य

These sounds are produced with the tongue near or against the teeth.

📍 Place: Teeth

Examples: त थ द ध न

Oṣṭhya — Labial

ओष्ठ्य

These sounds are produced using the lips.

📍 Place: Lips

Examples: प फ ब भ म

Other Articulations

अन्तःस्थ / ऊष्म

Some Sanskrit sounds involve other combinations of articulatory positions and airflow.

📍 Place: Depends on the sound

Examples: य र ल व श ष स ह
